# Physical Credibility Assessment

Physical credibility is problem-specific and follows case, mesh, execution and numerical credibility.

## Evidence categories

- dimensional/nondimensional regime (Re, Ma, Fr, Pr, Co, etc.) consistent with intended model;
- expected symmetry/asymmetry, direction, monotonic trends and limiting behavior;
- conservation and source/boundary balances;
- characteristic structures such as wake, shock, interface, circulation, pressure/temperature drop or rotating-frame behavior;
- comparison with analytical limits, trusted data, prior validated results or applicable engineering correlations;
- sensitivity to mesh, timestep, domain, model or averaging where required by the claim.

## Experience rules

A plausible contour is weak evidence. Agreement can be accidental if reference quantities, geometry or regimes differ. State applicability and uncertainty. When no trustworthy comparison/criterion exists, report `limited` with the strongest supported physical checks rather than inventing a tolerance.
